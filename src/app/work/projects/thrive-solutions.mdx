## Overview

Thrive Business Solutions handles bookkeeping and back-office work for labour hire, NDIS, healthcare, and construction operators around Brisbane — the kind of businesses that outgrow a generalist bookkeeper somewhere around the fiftieth employee. The brand does not hedge. *More Than Just Bloody Good Bookkeepers* runs across the hero, and the build had to carry that confidence while holding nine services, a growing article library, and a sales team that wants new pages live the week they ask for them.

## Content Architecture

The nine offerings — bookkeeping and BAS, payroll, forensic services, debtor finance, and the rest — are a Payload collection rendered through a single `services/[slug]` route, not nine hand-built pages. Each composes the same modular blocks: problem statement, value proposition, testimonial, related services, closing CTA. Articles under `resources/[slug]` work the same way.

The point of that structure is what it costs to grow. A tenth service is a CMS entry written by the person who sells it, not a ticket, an estimate, and a deployment.

## Front-End

Next.js App Router with Payload running inside the same application, so content is queried locally instead of over a network hop, and pages ship server-rendered with media served through Payload's own handler. The enquiry form mirrors the service catalogue in its dropdown, which means a lead arrives already sorted into the part of the business that should answer it.
