## Overview

Whitecomb Range is a private property in the New Zealand high country that sells three things which only look alike from a distance: somewhere to sleep, something to learn, and something to compete in. Each one answers "is this available?" differently, and none of them fit a standard product page.

## Three Product Lines, One Flow

Accommodation spans The Lodge, Norman's Hut, and The Tram Car — a modern group lodge, a traditional high-country hut sleeping seven, and a back-country tram car with a pot-belly stove and a stack of firewood. Different capacities, different seasons, different guests.

Training courses are multi-day, capped by headcount, and bundle meals and beds into the price — so a course booking has to reserve accommodation as well as a seat. Competitive shooting events run to a fixed calendar and are handled by The Events Calendar rather than forced into a product model that was never going to fit them.

The real work was making all three arrive at the same enquiry and checkout path, so a visitor never has to know which system is answering.

## Build

A custom theme rather than a page-builder, PHP templating with Tailwind over the top, styled mobile-first because that is how the audience actually books. WooCommerce now runs live checkout through Stripe, so the property takes payment directly instead of trading emails.
