## Overview

A wedding invitation is a link that gets forwarded — into a group chat, onto a lock screen, to a relative on a bad connection. So the entire build optimises for one path: someone taps a URL they did not ask for and needs the date, the place, and the RSVP before their attention runs out.

Statically rendered with Next.js and served from Vercel, which makes it close to instant on a phone and gives it a clean preview card when it lands in a messaging app. A small project with a narrow brief, and a good reminder that the right architecture is sometimes just *no server at all*.
